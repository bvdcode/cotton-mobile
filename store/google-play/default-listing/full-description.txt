Keep your Android folders connected to your Cotton Cloud — securely and on your terms.

Choose a cloud folder, pair it with a folder on your device, and decide how files should move.

• Upload new and changed files from Android.
• Sync changes in both directions.
• Review destructive actions before anything is removed.
• Keep local originals, or remove them only after a confirmed upload.
• Run, pause, resume, refresh, or stop each sync folder independently.

Connect by entering your Cotton Cloud URL and approve access securely in your browser. Cotton Cloud restores your session on the next launch, while sign-out clears local session data when you need it.

Cotton Cloud for Android is currently available through testing tracks while we continue refining the sync experience.
